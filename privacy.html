This Privacy Policy describes how Travis Pugh, Broker | TX Lic #463159-B (“we,” “us,” or “our”), collect, use, and share information when you use our website, our viewing-request chat, our short viewing form, and our SMS appointment program.

Information we collect

When you use the chat or short form, we may collect the information you provide, such as your name, mobile number, email address, the community or builder you are asking about, a requested viewing time, your messages, and your responses to the required consent boxes. We also collect records needed to honor a STOP or HELP request and to operate the appointment program.

How we use information

We use this information to respond to your inquiry, follow up as Travis Pugh, Broker, record your requested viewing time, complete the short confirmation steps for a showing, send related appointment texts or calls you agreed to, and register you with a builder only if you give permission to do that.

We do not use your SMS opt-in to send a recurring marketing or promotions program. Appointment texts are about your inquiry, requested viewing times, and related appointment follow-up, as described in our SMS Terms.

SMS and text-message program

If you check the text/call consent box and submit the form or complete the same consents in chat, you agree that we may text the mobile number you provide about your inquiry, requested viewing times, and related appointment follow-up.

Message frequency varies. For a typical viewing request you may receive a requested-time text, reminders about confirmation steps, a cancel notice if a requested time is canceled, a morning-of reminder if the showing is confirmed, and a post-tour follow-up only if Travis approves that specific message.

Msg & data rates may apply.

We do not sell or share your SMS opt-in data or personal information with third parties for marketing purposes.

You may reply STOP to opt out of texts at any time, or HELP for help. After we process a STOP request, we will not send further appointment texts to that number, except a brief confirmation that we received the request. You may also contact us at 214-301-8071 or travispugh20@gmail.com.

How we share information

We do not sell your phone number. We share your information with a home builder only if you give permission to register you with that builder so a viewing can be set up in Travis Pugh, Broker’s name.

We may use service providers that help us operate the website, form, chat, and SMS program (for example, a text-messaging provider). Those providers are used to deliver the service, not to buy your number for their own marketing.

We may also disclose information if we believe we are required to do so by law, or to protect rights, safety, or an existing appointment matter.

Retention

We keep chat and form records so Travis Pugh, Broker, can follow up, honor opt-out requests, and meet legal or professional record-keeping duties. The exact retention period is not set in this policy and may be updated after legal review.
